#Space Shooter

##About
Play as the pilot of the Space Federation and take on the dangerous Aliens! The game has three spaceships for you to choose from, and three levels you can play.

![Screenshot](/Public/Images/Image_4.png "Gameplay")
![Screenshot](/Public/Images/Image_5.png "Gameplay")

Walk out of the asteroid field, fight in a space battle, and risk a dangerous solo mission to defeat the alien leviathan! 

![Screenshot](/Public/Images/Image_1.png "Gameplay")
![Screenshot](/Public/Images/Image_3.png "Gameplay")

This is a learning project made by me with free assets. There are three levels and three spaceships with different characteristics for you to try.

##Setup
- Android: Download **Public/Space Shooter.apk** and install
- Windows: Download and unpack **Public/Space Shooter.zip** and run **Space Shooter.exe** file
